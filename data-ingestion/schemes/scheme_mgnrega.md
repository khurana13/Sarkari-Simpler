# MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act)

## Overview
MGNREGA is India's largest social protection scheme and the world's largest public works program. It guarantees the "Right to Work" by providing at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.

## Official Details
- **Enacted**: September 7, 2005 (NREGA Act 2005)
- **Renamed**: October 2, 2009 (as MGNREGA)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Rural Development
- **Official Portal**: [https://nrega.nic.in/](https://nrega.nic.in/)
- **Helpline**: Toll-Free Number varies by state

## Benefits

### Employment Guarantee
- **Days Guaranteed**: Minimum 100 days of wage employment per household per year
- **Work Provision**: Within 15 days of application, else unemployment allowance payable
- **Work Location**: Within 5 km radius of residence (else 10% extra wage)
- **Wage Payment**: Within 15 days of work completion

### Current Wage Rates (2024-25)
Wage rates vary by state (₹221 to ₹374 per day). Examples:
- **Haryana**: ₹374/day
- **Kerala**: ₹354/day
- **Karnataka**: ₹342/day
- **Maharashtra**: ₹295/day
- **Uttar Pradesh**: ₹230/day
- **Bihar**: ₹221/day

### Additional Benefits
- Equal wages for men and women
- Crèche facility if 5+ children at worksite
- Shade and drinking water at worksites
- Medical facilities for worksite accidents
- Unemployment allowance if work not provided

## Eligibility Criteria

### Simple Eligibility
- **Who**: Any adult (18+ years) rural household member
- **Residence**: Residing in rural areas
- **Work Type**: Willing to do unskilled manual labor
- **Registration**: Must apply for Job Card

### No Restrictions On
- Caste, religion, gender
- Landholding status (both landed and landless)
- Educational qualification
- Income level

## How to Apply

### Job Card Registration
1. **Apply at Gram Panchayat**:
   - Visit local GP office
   - Submit application with photo and proof of residence
   - Free of cost

2. **Required Documents**:
   - Address proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID/Ration Card)
   - Recent passport-size photograph
   - Age proof (for all adult members)

3. **Job Card Issuance**:
   - Issued within 15 days of application
   - Contains details of all adult household members
   - Unique Job Card number for tracking

### Demanding Work
1. **Written Application**: Submit to Gram Panchayat or Program Officer
2. **Receipt**: Dated receipt issued immediately
3. **Work Allocation**: Within 15 days from date of application
4. **Work Location**: Preferably within 5 km of residence

### Online Services
- **Job Card Download**: Available on [https://nrega.nic.in/](https://nrega.nic.in/)
- **Work Demand**: Can be submitted online in some states
- **Payment Status**: Check wage payment status online
- **Transparency**: All Job Cards, muster rolls, payments are public

## Types of Permitted Works

### Water Conservation
- Water harvesting structures
- Irrigation canals and tanks
- Drought proofing activities

### Land Development
- Land development on SC/ST/IAY beneficiaries' land
- Horticulture and plantation on public lands
- Flood control and drainage works

### Rural Connectivity
- Roads connecting villages to main roads
- Culverts and village infrastructure

### Other Works
- Anganwadi, playground, cremation ground construction
- Any other work notified by Central/State Government

## Payment Process

### Wage Payment
- **Mode**: Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) to Job Card holder's account
- **Timeline**: Within 15 days of work completion
- **Delay Compensation**: Daily compensation @ 0.05% of unpaid wages for delays
- **Account Linking**: Bank/Post Office account linked to Job Card

### Checking Payment Status
1. Visit [https://nrega.nic.in/](https://nrega.nic.in/)
2. Select "Job Cards" → State → District → Block → Panchayat
3. Search Job Card by number/name
4. View work details and payment history

## Key Rights of Workers

### Legal Entitlements
- Work within 15 days of demand application
- Unemployment allowance if work not provided within 15 days
- Payment within 15 days of work completion
- Equal wages for men and women
- Facilities at worksite (water, shade, first aid)
- Compensation for accidents/injuries

### Grievance Redressal
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: State-specific numbers
- **Online Complaints**: Via MGNREGA portal
- **Social Audit**: Regular public audits
- **Vigilance Committee**: At GP level

## Impact
- **Employment Generated**: Over 300 crore person-days annually
- **Workers**: ~5-6 crore households participate annually
- **Women Participation**: ~55% of total person-days
- **Rural Assets**: Millions of durable assets created
- **Poverty Reduction**: Significant impact on rural poverty and distress migration

## Technology Integration
- **MIS Portal**: Complete transparency of all data
- **NREGASoft**: Workflow management system
- **Geo-MGNREGA**: Geo-tagging of assets
- **Aadhaar-Based Payment System (ABPS)**: Direct payment with biometric authentication
- **Mobile App**: MGNREGA mobile app for workers
