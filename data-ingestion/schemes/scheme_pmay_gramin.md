# PM-AWAS Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) is a flagship scheme to achieve the objective of "Housing for All" by providing pucca houses with basic amenities to all homeless and households living in kutcha and dilapidated houses in rural areas.

## Official Details
- **Launch Date**: April 2016 (restructured from IAY)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Rural Development
- **Official Portal**: [https://pmayg.nic.in/](https://pmayg.nic.in/)
- **Target**: 2.95 crore houses by 2024

## Benefits

### Financial Assistance
- **Plains Areas**: ₹1,20,000 per unit
- **Hilly States/Difficult Areas/IAP Districts**: ₹1,30,000 per unit
- **Additional Support**: 90/95 days of unskilled wage employment from MGNREGA for construction

### House Specifications
- Minimum carpet area: 25 sq. meters (with provision for future expansion)
- Hygienic cooking space
- Clean drinking water facility
- Sanitary latrine
- Electricity connection (under Saubhagya scheme)
- LPG connection (under Ujjwala scheme)

## Eligibility Criteria

### Primary Eligibility (SECC 2011 Based)
Beneficiaries are identified from SECC 2011 data based on:
- Households without shelter
- Destitute/living on alms
- Manual scavenger families
- Primitive tribal groups
- Legally released bonded labor
- **Automatic Inclusion**:
  - SC/ST households with at least one room
  - Households without adult members (16-59 years)
  - Female-headed households with no adult male member
  - Disabled member and no able-bodied adult
  - Landless households deriving major income from manual casual labor

### Selection Process
- Beneficiaries identified by Gram Sabha from SECC list
- Priority to houseless, then kutcha houses, then 1-2 room kutcha
- Transparent selection through community participation
- Permanent waitlist for continuous selection

## How to Apply

### Beneficiary Identification
1. **Selection by Gram Sabha**: Beneficiaries selected from SECC 2011 list
2. **Verification**: Details verified by local authorities
3. **Approval**: Sanctioned by district-level committee

### Not Self-Registration
> [!IMPORTANT]
> PMAY-G is **not a self-registration scheme**. Beneficiaries are identified by Gram Sabhas based on SECC 2011 data and housing deprivation parameters.

### Check Eligibility/Status
1. Visit [https://pmayg.nic.in/](https://pmayg.nic.in/)
2. Click "Stakeholders" → "IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary"
3. Enter Registration Number or search by details
4. View beneficiary status and payment details

### Required Documents (Post-Selection)
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank account with Aadhaar linkage (for DBT)
- Convergence with Swachh Bharat Mission for toilet
- Convergence with Ujjwala for LPG
- Convergence with Saubhagya for electricity

## Payment Structure
- **3 Installments**:
  - 1st Installment: After plinth completion
  - 2nd Installment: After roof/lintel level
  - 3rd Installment: After house completion
- All payments made directly to beneficiary's bank account

## Key Features

### Technology Integration
- **AwaasSoft**: Complete workflow management system
- **AwaasApp**: Mobile app for geo-tagging and monitoring
- **Photo Upload**: Mandatory at each stage
- **Transparency**: All data publicly available online

### Quality & Monitoring
- National Technical Support Agency (NTSA) for quality
- Geo-tagged photographs at multiple stages
- Social audit and community monitoring
- Grievance redressal mechanism

## Impact
- **Houses Completed**: Over 2.5 crore pucca houses since 2016
- **Rural Transformation**: Improved living conditions for poorest families
- **Women Empowerment**: House ownership in name of female head or joint ownership
- **Convergence**: Integration with Swachh Bharat, Ujjwala, MGNREGA

## Key Features for Voice Agent

- **Status Check**: Can verify using registration number or personal details
