# PM-GKAY (Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PM-GKAY) is a food security scheme launched to ensure food security for the poorest and most vulnerable sections during the COVID-19 pandemic and beyond. It provides free foodgrains in addition to the regular subsidized allocation under the National Food Security Act (NFSA).

## Official Details
- **Launch Date**: March 2020 (COVID-19 relief)
- **Extended**: Multiple extensions; currently ongoing
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **Official Portal**: [https://dfpd.gov.in/](https://dfpd.gov.in/)
- **Coverage**: Under National Food Security Act, 2013

## Benefits

### Foodgrain Allocation
- **Free Foodgrains**: 5 kg per person per month
- **Grains Provided**: Rice or Wheat (based on state preference)
- **Additional to NFSA**: Over and above regular NFSA entitlement

### Total Entitlement (NFSA + PM-GKAY)
- **AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana)**: 35 kg/family/month (NFSA) + 5 kg/person/month (PM-GKAY)
- **PHH (Priority Household)**: 5 kg/person/month (NFSA) + 5 kg/person/month (PM-GKAY)

### Cost
- **PM-GKAY Allocation**: Completely free
- **NFSA Allocation**: Highly subsidized
  - Rice: ₹3/kg
  - Wheat: ₹2/kg
  - Coarse grains: ₹1/kg

## Eligibility Criteria

### Who is Covered
PM-GKAY benefits are **automatically provided** to all NFSA beneficiaries:

#### AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana) Cardholders
- Poorest of the poor families
- Annual household income criteria as per state
- Typically: destitute, homeless, disabled, aged persons without support

#### PHH (Priority Household) Cardholders
- Families not covered under AAY
- Income-based criteria (state-specific)
- Generally households below poverty line

### Total Coverage
- **Beneficiaries**: ~80 crore (800 million) individuals
- **Coverage**: ~67% of India's population
- **Families**: ~23 crore ration card holders

## How to Avail Benefits

### Automatic Provision
> [!IMPORTANT]
> PM-GKAY is **not a separate scheme** requiring new application. If you have an **NFSA ration card** (AAY or PHH), you automatically receive PM-GKAY benefits.

### Steps to Receive Grains
1. **Must Have**: Valid NFSA ration card (AAY/PHH)
2. **Visit**: Allocated Fair Price Shop (FPS/ration shop)
3. **Identify**: Using Aadhaar-based biometric authentication or ration card
4. **Collect**: Regular NFSA quota + PM-GKAY free grains
5. **No Payment**: PM-GKAY grains are completely free

### Check Eligibility/Entitlement
- **State Food Department Portal**: Each state has online portal
- **Ration Card Details**: Check with local FPS
- **One Nation One Ration Card**: [https://www.nfsa.gov.in/](https://www.nfsa.gov.in/)

## New Ration Card Application

### If You Don't Have Ration Card
1. **Apply at**: District Food & Civil Supplies Office or online state portal
2. **Required Documents**:
   - Address proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID/Utility bill)
   - Income certificate
   - Family composition details
   - BPL certificate (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar cards of all family members

3. **Process**:
   - Submit application with documents
   - Verification by authorities
   - Ration card issued (typically 30-60 days)

### State Portals for Ration Card
Different states have different portals:
- **Delhi**: [https://nfs.delhi.gov.in/](https://nfs.delhi.gov.in/)
- **Uttar Pradesh**: [https://fcs.up.gov.in/](https://fcs.up.gov.in/)
- **Maharashtra**: [https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in/](https://rcms.mahafood.gov.in/)
- **Karnataka**: [https://ahara.kar.nic.in/](https://ahara.kar.nic.in/)
- And others (visit state food department website)

## One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)

### Portability Feature
- **Benefit**: Can collect ration from any FPS in India
- **Coverage**: Implemented across all 36 states/UTs
- **Identification**: Aadhaar-based biometric authentication
- **Advantage**: Helps migrant workers and mobile population

### How ONORC Works
1. Have valid NFSA ration card from home state
2. Visit any FPS in any state
3. Authenticate with Aadhaar biometric
4. Collect entitled foodgrains
5. Allocation automatically adjusted in home state

## Checking Ration Card Status

### Online Verification
1. Visit state food department website
2. Enter ration card number
3. View:
   - Card validity
   - Family members
   - Monthly entitlement
   - Last transaction details
   - Allocated FPS

### SMS Services
Many states offer SMS-based services:
- Send ration card number to designated number
- Receive entitlement and last transaction details

## Impact
- **COVID-19 Relief**: Critical support during pandemic lockdowns
- **Food Security**: Ensured zero hunger for vulnerable families
- **Distribution**: Over 1,000+ lakh metric tonnes distributed since inception
- **Coverage**: Reaching ~80 crore beneficiaries
- **Economic Impact**: Savings of ~₹34,000 crore annually for beneficiaries

## Recent Updates
- **Extension**: PM-GKAY extended multiple times based on need
- **Integration**: Seamless integration with NFSA distribution
- **Technology**: ePoS (electronic Point of Sale) at FPS for transparency
- **Aadhaar Seeding**: Linking ration cards with Aadhaar for authentication
