# PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) is the flagship skill training scheme of the Government of India, implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. It aims to enable Indian youth to take up industry-relevant skill training that will help them secure employment or become self-employed.

## Official Details
- **Launch Date**: 2015 (PMKVY 4.0 active from 2022–2026)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship
- **Official Portal**: [https://www.pmkvyofficial.org/](https://www.pmkvyofficial.org/)
- **Helpline**: 1800-123-9626 (Toll-Free)

## Benefits

### Free Skill Training
- Short-term training programmes (150–300 hours)
- Covers 300+ job roles across 38 sectors
- Training at certified Training Partners / Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Kendras

### Monetary Reward
- **₹500 to ₹1,500** post-training monetary reward on successful assessment
- Reward deposited directly to bank account

### Industry-Recognized Certification
- Certificate recognized by industry under National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF)
- Valid for jobs in India and abroad

### Placement Assistance
- Training Partners required to provide placement support
- Job fairs organized by National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)

## Eligibility
- Indian citizens, unemployed or school/college dropouts
- Age: 15–45 years (varies by sector)
- Aadhaar card and bank account required

## How to Apply
1. Visit [https://www.pmkvyofficial.org/](https://www.pmkvyofficial.org/)
2. Click "Find a Training Centre" and select your district
3. Enroll at nearest training centre with Aadhaar and bank details
4. Complete training and appear for assessment
5. Receive certification and monetary reward
