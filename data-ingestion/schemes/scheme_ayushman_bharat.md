# Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

## Overview
Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is the world's largest health insurance/assurance scheme, providing health cover to poor and vulnerable families. It is a centrally sponsored scheme with 60:40 funding between Centre and States (90:10 for North-Eastern and Himalayan states).

## Official Details
- **Launch Date**: September 23, 2018
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
- **Implementing Agency**: National Health Authority (NHA)
- **Official Portal**: [https://pmjay.gov.in/](https://pmjay.gov.in/)
- **Helpline**: 14555

## Benefits

### Health Coverage
- **Sum Insured**: ₹5 lakh per family per year
- **Coverage**: Secondary and tertiary care hospitalization
- **Family Definition**: No restriction on family size, age, or gender
- **Coverage Type**: Portable across India - can avail at any empanelled hospital

### Covered Services
- **Pre-Hospitalization**: Up to 3 days before admission
- **Post-Hospitalization**: Up to 15 days after discharge
- **Medical Procedures**: 1,949+ medical packages covering surgeries, treatments, diagnostics
- **Transport Allowance**: Included in package rates
- **Free Services**: No cost for treatment at empanelled hospitals

### Key Features
- Cashless and paperless service at hospitals
- No cap on family size or age
- Pre-existing diseases covered from day one
- Includes both public and private empanelled hospitals

## Eligibility Criteria

### Automatic Eligibility (SECC 2011)
Families are identified based on deprivation and occupational criteria from SECC 2011:

**Deprivation Criteria (Rural):**
- Only one room with kutcha walls and roof
- No adult member (16-59 years)
- Female-headed household with no adult male member (16-59 years)
- Disabled member and no able-bodied adult member
- SC/ST household
- Landless households deriving major income from manual casual labor

**Occupational Criteria (Rural):** 
Automatically included if household has:
- Manual scavenger families
- Primitive tribal groups
- Legally released bonded labor
- Homeless/living on alms
- Destitute/beggars

**Urban Categories:**
- Ragpickers, street vendors, cobblers
- Construction workers, plumbers, welders
- Domestic workers, washermen, rickshaw pullers
- Transport workers, shop workers
- And other specified 11 occupational categories

## How to Check Eligibility

### Online Verification
1. Visit [https://pmjay.gov.in/](https://pmjay.gov.in/)
2. Click "Am I Eligible"
3. Enter mobile number and OTP
4. Search by:
   - HHD Number (from ration card)
   - Name and other details
   - Ration Card Number
   - Mobile Number
5. View eligibility status

### Offline Verification
- Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
- Visit empanelled hospital help desk
- Contact State Health Agency office
- Call helpline: 14555

## How to Get Ayushman Card

### E-Card Generation
1. **At Hospital**: Visit any empanelled hospital with identity proof
2. **At CSC**: Visit Common Service Centre
3. **Online**: Through beneficiary portal (if pre-registered)

### Required Documents
- **Identity Proof** (any one):
  - Aadhaar Card (preferred)
  - Voter ID
  - Ration Card
  - Driving License
  - PAN Card
  - Government photo ID

### E-Card Features
- QR code with beneficiary details
- Valid at any empanelled hospital across India
- Can be carried digitally on phone
- Free of cost

## Using the Scheme

### Treatment Process
1. **Check Eligibility**: Verify online or at hospital
2. **Choose Hospital**: Find empanelled hospital via PMJAY app/website
3. **Admission**: Show Ayushman Card or identity proof
4. **Verification**: Hospital verifies eligibility
5. **Treatment**: Receive cashless treatment
6. **Discharge**: No payment required (within package)

### Finding Hospitals
- **Website**: [https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in/](https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in/)
- **Mobile App**: "Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY" app
- **Helpline**: 14555
- **Filters**: Search by location, specialty, hospital type

## Impact
- **Coverage**: Over 12 crore (120 million) families (~50 crore individuals)
- **Empanelled Hospitals**: 27,000+ public and private hospitals
- **Treatments**: Over 5 crore treatments authorized since launch
- **Claim Value**: Over ₹70,000 crore disbursed
- **Gender Impact**: ~45% beneficiaries are women

## State-Specific Schemes
Many states have combined PM-JAY with their own schemes:
- **Delhi**: Ayushman Bharat - Delhi scheme (expanded coverage)
- **Tamil Nadu**: Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme
- **Andhra Pradesh**: Aarogyasri (integrated)
- **Telangana**: Aarogyasri (integrated)
