# PM-Kisan (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

## Overview
PM-Kisan is a central sector scheme that provides direct income support to landholding farmers across India. The scheme aims to supplement the financial needs of farmers for procuring various agricultural inputs and meeting their domestic requirements.

## Official Details
- **Launch Date**: February 2019
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
- **Official Portal**: [https://pmkisan.gov.in/](https://pmkisan.gov.in/)
- **Scheme Type**: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

## Benefits
- **Financial Support**: ₹6,000 per year per family
- **Payment Mode**: Three equal installments of ₹2,000 each
- **Payment Frequency**: Every 4 months
- **Transfer Method**: Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) to beneficiary's bank account

## Eligibility Criteria

### Eligible Beneficiaries
- All landholding farmer families (irrespective of size of landholding)
- Farmer families defined as: husband, wife, and minor children who own cultivable land

### Exclusion Criteria
The following categories are **not eligible**:
- Institutional landholders
- Farmer families with one or more members who are:
  - Constitutional post holders (present or former)
  - Present or former Ministers/State Ministers
  - Present or former Members of Parliament/Legislative Assembly/Legislative Council
  - Present or former Mayors of Municipal Corporations
  - Present or former Chairpersons of District Panchayats
  - Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government, PSUs, and autonomous bodies (excluding Multi-Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D)
  - All superannuated/retired pensioners with monthly pension ≥ ₹10,000
  - Professionals like doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, architects
  - Income tax payers in the last assessment year

## How to Apply

### Registration Process
1. **Self-Registration**: Visit [https://pmkisan.gov.in/](https://pmkisan.gov.in/) and click on "Farmers Corner"
2. **Through CSC**: Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
3. **Through Local Records**: Contact local Patwari/Revenue Officer/Nodal Officer

### Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for DBT)
- Bank account details (with IFSC code)
- Land ownership records (Khata/Khasra numbers)
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

### Application Steps
1. Visit PM-Kisan portal or CSC
2. Select "Farmers Corner" → "New Farmer Registration"
3. Enter Aadhaar number and select state
4. Fill in personal details, bank account, and land details
5. Submit application and save acknowledgment number

## Verification & Payment
- Application verified by local revenue authorities
- Once approved, beneficiary name appears in beneficiary list
- Payments made directly to bank account every 4 months

## Check Application Status
- Visit [https://pmkisan.gov.in/](https://pmkisan.gov.in/)
- Click "Farmers Corner" → "Beneficiary Status"
- Enter Aadhaar/Account Number/Mobile Number
- View registration status and payment history

## Impact
- **Coverage**: Over 11 crore (110 million) farmer families
- **Total Disbursement**: Over ₹2.8 lakh crore transferred since inception
- **Rural Reach**: Significant impact on rural economy and farmer welfare
