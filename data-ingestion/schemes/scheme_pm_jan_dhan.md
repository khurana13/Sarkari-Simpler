# PM Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is the world's largest financial inclusion initiative, ensuring access to financial services—banking, savings, remittance, credit, insurance, and pension—for all citizens, especially the unbanked poor.

## Official Details
- **Launch Date**: August 28, 2014
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Finance
- **Official Portal**: [https://pmjdy.gov.in/](https://pmjdy.gov.in/)
- **Helpline**: 1800-11-0001 (Toll-Free)

## Benefits

### Zero-Balance Account
- Open a savings bank account with **zero minimum balance**
- Interest earned on deposits as per savings account norms
- Access to banking services at bank branches, ATMs, and BCs

### RuPay Debit Card
- **Free RuPay Debit Card** issued to every account holder
- **₹2 lakh accidental insurance** cover (for accounts opened after 28.8.2018)
- **₹30,000 life insurance** cover (for eligible accounts)

### Overdraft Facility
- **Up to ₹10,000** overdraft available to eligible account holders
- No collateral required for overdraft up to ₹10,000

### Direct Benefit Transfer
- Government subsidies and benefits transferred directly into PMJDY accounts
- Eliminates middlemen and reduces leakage

## Eligibility
- Any Indian citizen aged 10 years or above can open an account
- No minimum balance required
- Available at all public sector banks, regional rural banks, and cooperative banks

## How to Open an Account
1. Visit nearest bank branch or Business Correspondent (BC)
2. Fill in account opening form
3. Provide Aadhaar card or any valid photo ID
4. Account opened immediately; RuPay card delivered within 15 days

## Required Documents
- **Aadhaar Card** (preferred) OR
- Voter ID, Driving License, PAN Card, or any government-issued photo ID
- Passport-size photograph
