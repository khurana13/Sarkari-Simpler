# Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM)

## Overview
Jal Jeevan Mission aims to provide safe and adequate drinking water through individual household tap connections (FHTC - Functional Household Tap Connection) to every rural household in India by 2024. The mission is based on a community-centric approach.

## Official Details
- **Launch Date**: August 15, 2019
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Jal Shakti
- **Official Portal**: [https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/](https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/)
- **Helpline**: 1800-180-1551 (Toll-Free)

## Benefits

### Tap Water Connection
- **Free piped water connection** to every rural household
- Minimum **55 litres per person per day** of potable water
- Clean water reduces waterborne diseases significantly

### Priority Groups
- SC/ST households
- Households with disabled members
- Widowed women-headed households
- PMAY (Gramin) beneficiaries get priority

## Coverage
- **Target**: 19.3 crore rural households
- **Achieved**: 15+ crore connections (as of 2024)
- Covers all villages across India

## Implementation
- Gram Panchayat and Village Water & Sanitation Committees (VWSC) manage local infrastructure
- States contribute 10% (Northeast/Himalayan states contribute 10%)
- Centre contributes 90% for special category states, 50% for others

## How to Check Status
1. Visit [https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/](https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/)
2. Use "Dashboard" to track state/district/village-level progress
3. Contact local Gram Panchayat or district Jal Shakti officer

## Key Features for Voice Agent
- **Free Connection**: Government installs tap at your home at no cost
- **Clean Water**: Treated, potable water delivered at doorstep
- **Priority**: SC/ST and vulnerable households get priority
- **Common Queries**: Connection status, how to apply, grievance redressal
